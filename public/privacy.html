Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Last updated 15 October 2026

CarbonZero (“we”, “us”) is a free, non-commercial student project built by Team Nigeshhh (Group 8) in Kochi, Kerala, India. It helps people estimate their household carbon footprint, explore ways to reduce it, build a reduction plan and, if they choose, save that plan to their own Google Drive. This policy explains what information the website at carbon-footprint-calc-v5.vercel.app (the “Service”) handles, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You can use the calculator, solutions hub and planner without signing in. Those numbers are processed in your browser.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account ID.
  • Saving to Google Drive only creates files you ask us to create. We can't see the rest of your Drive.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.
  • You can disconnect CarbonZero from your Google Account at any time, and email us to delete what we hold.

1. Information we collect

a. Calculator and planner inputs

The figures you enter (for example electricity use, LPG, vehicle mileage, flights and diet) and the plan you build are processed in your browser to calculate your results. We don't send them to our servers or store them in a database. They leave your device only when you choose to export a plan, save it to Google Drive, or type them into the AI assistant.

b. Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose “Connect” or “Sign in with Google”, we use Firebase Authentication (a Google service) to sign you in. We receive your name, email address, profile photo URL and a unique Google account identifier. Firebase keeps a user record for our project (including sign-in dates), and keeps you signed in using your browser’s storage. We use this information only to show that you are signed in, to label the plans you save, and to keep your assistant conversation linked to your account.

c. Google Drive (optional)

When you choose to save a plan to Google Drive, we ask for the drive.file permission, which only lets CarbonZero work with files that CarbonZero itself creates (or that you explicitly open with it). With that permission we:

  • create your reduction plan as a new file in your Drive; and
  • list the names, dates and links of plan files CarbonZero created, so you can find your earlier plans.

We cannot see, read, change or delete any other files in your Drive. The temporary access token Google gives us is kept only in your browser’s memory, expires after about an hour, and is never stored on our servers or in our database.

d. AI assistant messages

If you use the CarbonZero assistant, the messages you type and the conversation so far are sent through our server (a Vercel serverless function) to Google’s Gemini API to generate a reply. We don’t send your name, email address or Google Drive content to Gemini. If you are signed in, the conversation is also saved to Cloud Firestore under your account, where our security rules allow only you to read or write it. Please don’t share sensitive personal information in the chat.

e. Technical data

Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) and the providers of the fonts (Google Fonts) and photographs (Unsplash) we display automatically receive standard request information such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep it in short-term logs for security and operations. We don’t use this information to identify or profile you.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • calculate and display your footprint, plan and results;
  • sign you in and show your account in the navigation bar;
  • save your plan to your Google Drive and show your previously saved plans when you ask;
  • answer your questions in the AI assistant and keep your conversation for you while signed in; and
  • keep the Service working, secure and free of abuse.

We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, build marketing profiles, or use it to make automated decisions about you.

3. How we handle Google user data

CarbonZero’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular:

  • We use your Google profile information and Drive access only to provide the user-facing features described above.
  • We don’t transfer Google user data to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with the law, or with your explicit consent.
  • We don’t use Google user data for advertising, and we don’t sell it.
  • We don’t let humans read Google user data unless you ask us to (for example in a support request), it is needed for security or to comply with the law.
  • We don’t use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

5. Cookies and browser storage

We don’t use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The Service stores a small amount of data in your browser to work properly:

  • your light or dark theme preference (carbonzero_theme in local storage); and
  • if you sign in, Firebase’s sign-in session, so you stay signed in between visits.

You can clear these at any time in your browser settings. Signing out removes the sign-in session.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Calculator and planner inputs stay in your browser and are cleared when you close or reload the page.
  • Plan files in Google Drive belong to you and stay there until you delete them in Drive. Disconnecting CarbonZero does not delete them.
  • Drive access tokens are kept in browser memory only and expire after about an hour.
  • Your account record and saved assistant conversation are kept while the Service is running so you can return to them. Email us and we will delete them within 30 days. If the project is shut down, we will delete them.
  • Hosting logs are kept by Vercel for a short period under its own retention settings.

To stop CarbonZero accessing your Google Account, remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections.

7. Security

The Service is served only over HTTPS. Your saved conversation is protected by Firestore security rules so that only your signed-in account can access it, and Drive access is limited to the narrow drive.file permission. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly on any issue reported to us.

8. Children

CarbonZero is not directed at children under 13, and they should not sign in or use the AI assistant. If you are under 18, please use the Service with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

You can always:

  • use the calculator, solutions and planner without signing in;
  • download your plan to your own device instead of saving it to Drive;
  • sign out, or revoke CarbonZero’s access in your Google Account settings; and
  • ask us for a copy of, a correction to, or deletion of the information we hold about you.

Depending on where you live, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have further rights such as to withdraw consent or to raise a grievance. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Where data is processed

We are based in India. Our service providers, Google and Vercel, operate infrastructure in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live, subject to their safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. If a change materially affects how we use Google user data, we will ask for your consent before applying it.

12. Contact us
