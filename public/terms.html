2. What CarbonZero is

CarbonZero lets you estimate your household carbon footprint, explore reduction ideas and build a reduction plan. Calculations use published factors, such as the Central Electricity Authority (CEA India) grid emission factor, and methods based on the Greenhouse Gas Protocol.

All results, savings and projections are estimates for educational purposes. They depend on your inputs and on simplifying assumptions, and may not match your actual emissions, costs or savings. CarbonZero is not financial, engineering, legal or other professional advice, and it is not a certified carbon audit or offset. Please consult a qualified professional before making significant decisions, such as installing equipment.
